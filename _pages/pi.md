Dr. Cuong Tuan Nguyen is a junior lecturer and academic coordinator of the Computer Science Program at Vietnamese-German University (VGU). He received his B.Eng. in Computer Engineering from Ho Chi Minh University of Technology, and his M.Sc. and Ph.D. in Computer Science from Tokyo University of Agriculture and Technology (TUAT).

Before joining VGU, he was an assistant professor at TUAT, an associate professor at the WACOM-TUAT Joint Research Laboratory, and a lecturer/researcher at FPT University Ho Chi Minh City campus.

## Research interests

- Handwritten mathematical expression, Japanese text, and English text recognition.
- Temporal classification, structural parsing, graph algorithms, and attention models.
- Weakly supervised learning and object detection for handwritten text recognition.
- Tracking algorithms and their applications.

## Academic activities

He co-organized the VOHTR2018 competition on Vietnamese online handwriting recognition and the CROHME 2023 competition on handwritten mathematical expression recognition.
