The Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (PAMI) Lab is a research group at Vietnamese-German University (VGU), led by <a href="{{ '/pi/' | relative_url }}">Dr. Cuong Tuan Nguyen</a>. We develop machine learning methods for understanding complex visual and structured data, with a particular focus on handwriting, document intelligence, and intelligent systems.

Our work connects fundamental models with practical applications in education, human-computer interaction, and language technologies.

Our research interests include:
- Temporal classification, structural parsing, graph algorithms, attention models for handwritten mathematical expression recognition.
- Weakly supervised models, object detection models for handwritten text recognition.
- Object detection models, tracking algorithms and their applications.
