## Two papers accepted at ICDAR 2026 and DAS 2026

We are pleased to announce that two papers from the PAMI Lab have been accepted for presentation at international conferences in 2026:

- **ICDAR 2026:** [Automated Character-Level Annotation for Historical Nom Documents via an Iterative Self-Updating Radical-Based Recognizer](https://icdar2026.org/index.php/accepted-papers/)
- **DAS 2026:** [Edge-Aware Relation Prediction for Symbol-Level Structure Refinement in Online Handwritten Mathematical Expressions](https://das2026.seecs.edu.pk/program.html)

Congratulations to all authors and collaborators!
