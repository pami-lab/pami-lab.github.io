# PAMI Lab Announces Two New Papers on Traffic Prediction and Person Anomaly Search

We are pleased to share two new research papers from the **Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (PAMI) Lab at Vietnamese-German University**, developed together with collaborators from GenAI4E Lab and partner universities.

These works involve several PAMI Lab members, including **Hieu Dinh Trung Pham, Khang Minh Le, Phuong Huu Vu Tran, Son Nguyen Minh Le, and Huy Minh Nhat Nguyen**, under the supervision of **Dr. Cuong Tuan Nguyen**, Principal Investigator of PAMI Lab, with **Huy Minh Nhat Nguyen** also serving as a mentor to the student researchers.

### GeoRoute: Geometry-Aware Hybrid Inference for Traffic Future-Frame Prediction

[**GeoRoute**](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09493v1) presents a geometry-aware hybrid inference framework for long-horizon traffic future-frame prediction. The work combines video generation with geometric refinement and view-conditioned routing to improve the consistency of predicted traffic scenes across different camera settings.

**PAMI Lab authors:** Khang Minh Le, Hieu Dinh Trung Pham, Phuong Huu Vu Tran, Son Nguyen Minh Le, Huy Minh Nhat Nguyen, and Dr. Cuong Tuan Nguyen, together with collaborators from other institutions.

The proposed system was evaluated in **AI City Challenge 2026 Track 5**, achieving **5th place on the official full-test leaderboard**.

### FaLCon: Facet-Anchored Retrieval with Late Consensus for Sim2Real Text-Based Person Anomaly Search

[**FaLCon**](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09474v1) addresses Sim2Real text-based person anomaly search with a coarse-to-fine vision-language retrieval framework. The approach combines facet-aware retrieval, multimodal reranking, semantic verification, and late consensus to better distinguish fine-grained human actions and attributes.

**PAMI Lab authors:** Hieu Dinh Trung Pham, Phuong Huu Vu Tran, Son Nguyen Minh Le, Khang Minh Le, Huy Minh Nhat Nguyen, and Dr. Cuong Tuan Nguyen, together with collaborators from other institutions.

Congratulations to all PAMI Lab members and collaborators involved in these works!

We would especially like to acknowledge **Dr. Cuong Tuan Nguyen for his supervision and research guidance, and Huy Minh Nhat Nguyen for mentoring and supporting the student researchers throughout these projects.**

We look forward to continuing our research in **computer vision, multimodal learning, video understanding, and intelligent transportation systems**.
