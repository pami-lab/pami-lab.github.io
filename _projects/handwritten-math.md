## Overview

This project develops methods for recognizing handwritten mathematical expressions. The work addresses both online recognition, where the sequence of pen strokes is available, and offline recognition, where the input is an image. Our approaches combine neural sequence models, symbol-level relations, global context, and grammar-based structural analysis to convert handwritten expressions into structured representations such as LaTeX.

The research has explored symbol segmentation and recognition, spatial-relation classification, relation-based representations, weakly supervised end-to-end recognition, and graph neural networks for expression structure recognition. Experiments use the CROHME datasets and related handwriting-recognition benchmarks.
